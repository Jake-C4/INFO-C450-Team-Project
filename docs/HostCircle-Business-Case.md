## 1. Problem

**Who is affected?**  
Groups of friends who regularly take turns hosting game nights, dinners, or other gatherings.

**What’s wrong today?**  
When a group regularly gets together, it can be hard to keep track of who hosted last, whose turn is next, and what everyone contributed. Over time, some people may end up hosting or contributing more than others.

**How will we know it worked?**  
The project will be successful if at least 80% of gatherings created during the initial evaluation period have a host and contributions recorded, allowing the group to track how hosting responsibilities are being shared.

## 2. Proposed Solution

HostCircle would give groups one place to organize their regular gatherings, keep track of who hosts, and record what each person contributes. This would make it easier for the group to share responsibilities and keep hosting fair over time.

## 3. Options Considered

| Option | Description | Pros | Cons |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Do nothing** | Continue keeping track of gatherings through group messages, memory, or other methods the group already uses. | No additional cost or new system to learn. | It can be difficult to remember who hosted or contributed in the past. |
| **Option A** | Use existing calendars, group chats, or shared lists to organize gatherings. | Uses tools the group may already have. | Information may be spread across different places and does not provide one clear record of hosting and contributions. |
| **Option B – Recommended** | Create HostCircle to organize gatherings and keep track of hosting and contributions in one place. | Gives the group one place to manage the rotation and see how responsibilities are being shared. | Requires development, testing, maintenance, and ongoing operating costs. |

## 4. Can We, and Is It Worth It?

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| **Will people actually use it?** | There is a reason for people who regularly host gatherings to use it because it gives them one place to keep track of hosting responsibilities. Actual use would need to be measured after the app is introduced. |
| **Can we build it with what we have?** | Yes. The first version can focus on the main purpose of organizing gatherings and keeping track of hosts and contributions without adding unnecessary features. |
| **Can it be done in time to matter?** | Yes, if the first version stays focused on organizing gatherings, tracking hosting turns, and recording contributions. |
| **What does it cost?** | There would be initial costs to develop and set up the app along with ongoing costs such as hosting, maintenance, and any services needed to keep it running. Exact costs would need to be determined before development. |
| **What do we gain?** | The main benefit is having one place to keep track of gatherings, hosting turns, and contributions. The number of gatherings with a recorded host and contributions can be measured to see whether people are actually using the app for its intended purpose. |

## 5. Recommendation

**Recommend moving forward with HostCircle.** The sponsor should approve the project because it addresses a specific problem for friend groups that regularly share hosting responsibilities. Creating HostCircle would give groups a simple way to organize gatherings, rotate hosting responsibilities, and track contributions fairly.
